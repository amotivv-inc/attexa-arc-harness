# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Attexa Arc Harness community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the integrity of the project;
- representing uncertainty, evidence, and implementation status honestly.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- sustained disruption of community discussions or events;
- knowingly fabricating tests, evidence, provenance, authorship, or verification;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, deceptive, or harmful.

Community leaders may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. Project spaces include repositories, issue trackers, review threads, discussion forums, community calls, events, and official social channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, deceptive, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to `conduct@amotivv.com`.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Community leaders will respect the privacy and security of reporters and affected participants.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will use these guidelines in determining consequences.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Use of inappropriate language or other behavior considered unprofessional or unwelcome.

**Consequence:** A private written warning that explains the nature and impact of the behavior. A public correction or apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. Interaction with involved people may be restricted for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from project interaction and public communication for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, deception, or other sustained harmful conduct.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from project interaction and representation.

## Human and AI-assisted participation

Attexa Arc Harness is explicitly concerned with collaboration among human, artificial, and deterministic performers. Contributions produced with AI or agent assistance are evaluated on technical merit, provenance, evidence, safety, and alignment with the project rather than rejected because of their origin.

The accountable contributor must not:

- use an agent to evade authorship, license, review, or conduct responsibilities;
- represent agent output as independently verified when it is not;
- publish hidden reasoning, credentials, private data, or confidential material;
- use automation to overwhelm maintainers, discussions, or review capacity.

Practical disclosure of material automation is encouraged. Private chain-of-thought is neither required nor desired. Structured artifacts, tests, limitations, and verifier findings are more useful.
