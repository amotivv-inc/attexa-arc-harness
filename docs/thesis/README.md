# The Work Should Remain

## Purpose

This directory records the public thesis lineage behind Attexa Arc Harness.

The narrative essays are intended for the Agent Anything blog. The repository records their conceptual role, publication status, relationship to the architecture, and explicit non-claims. It does not publish unfinished article drafts merely to demonstrate activity.

The four-part series is:

```text
The Work Is Fragmenting
        |
        v
The Workplace Should Outlive the Worker
        |
        v
The Work Should Teach the Next Worker
        |
        v
The Work Needs a Steward
```

Together they describe the problem, structural response, compounding result, and human practice behind Arc.

## Part 1: The Work Is Fragmenting

### Question

What is happening to organizations as work spreads across more people, agents, vendors, applications, and temporary sessions?

### Core claim

> **We are not trying to make every person and agent use one tool. We are trying to prevent the work from fracturing along with the tools.**

The essay should diagnose how an organization can produce more output while accumulating less shared understanding. It should not argue against AI acceleration or demand that all work move into one application.

### Relationship to Arc

This is the public problem statement for the project.

## Part 2: The Workplace Should Outlive the Worker

### Question

What kind of structure allows a critical body of work to remain understandable after its original participants are gone?

### Core claim

> **The system should not remain understandable only for as long as the people who built it remain available.**

The essay should use software and vendor-transition experience as its primary anchor while abstracting the pattern to other long-lived work. It should explain the counterfactual experience of entering a durable workplace and the practical value of starting that continuity now even when history is incomplete.

### Relationship to Arc

This is the public structural thesis:

> **The workplace persists. Intelligence visits.**

## Part 3: The Work Should Teach the Next Worker

### Question

How can present Work responsibly reconstruct missing understanding and create a better starting point for whoever follows?

### Core claim

> **The point is not to preserve every conversation. It is to ensure that each important episode of Work leaves the next worker with a better workplace.**

The essay should distinguish direct historical evidence, contemporaneous artifacts, attributed testimony, technical inference, current requirements, present decisions, and explicit unknowns. It should reject retroactive mythmaking.

### Relationship to Arc

This is the public explanation of accretion, truth classification, and continuity without requiring readers to begin with kernel mechanics.

## Part 4: The Work Needs a Steward

### Question

Why does durable continuity require a human practice even when the kernel and agents become highly capable?

### Core claim

> **Arc is not simply installed into an organization. Arc is practiced against a body of work.**

The essay should introduce Arc Practice and the Arc Practitioner as the role that helps an organization bound the work, recover the Situation, establish truth and authority, conduct real Work, accrete useful results, and test transfer.

It should emphasize:

> **The workplace should outlive every worker, including the practitioner who helped establish it.**

### Relationship to Arc

This is the public practice thesis and the conceptual foundation for practitioner pilots. It is not an announcement of a certification program.

## Shared non-claims

The series should not imply:

- complete capture of every decision or conversation;
- magical recovery of history that was never recorded;
- that persistence makes information true;
- that Arc eliminates the need for domain expertise or responsible authority;
- that the current prototype is production-ready;
- that the open-source repository already contains the runnable kernel;
- that an Arc Practitioner certification currently exists;
- that one method fits every body of work without adaptation.

## Arc building Arc

The essays are part of the project's lineage, not merely marketing collateral.

They explain why the project exists, what it intends to preserve, how useful understanding accumulates, and why stewardship matters. The public repository then turns those claims into operating instructions, implementation contracts, Work, evidence, and accepted project state.
